## New features

### Favorites
Mark the games you love as favorites, so they stand out everywhere.

- Right-click a game in **Profiles** and choose **Favorite profile** (or **Unfavorite profile** to remove it).
- Favorites shine with a **holographic foil** on their cover in the library.
- **Big Picture:** favorites have the foil too, and their own **Favorites** page between Home and Library. Press **X** on a game to add it to your favorites or remove it.
- **Remote Monitoring:** favorites have a **golden frame** on your phone.
