# arcora 1.0.0

A fresh look, a mode for the TV, playtime tracking from your phone and much more.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Choose a platform for every profile.** The platform is now a required field.
> No platforms to choose from? Open **Selection Lists** and add entries to **Platforms**.

> [!NOTE]
> arcora is now installed **per user**, without administrator rights. Your previous installation is removed and your data is taken over automatically.

---

## Highlights

### Big Picture mode
Your library in full screen for the TV, operated with a game controller, the keyboard or the mouse.

- Open it with the **controller icon** in the title bar.
- Browse your recently played games, the whole library and the search, start games and start or stop the monitoring.
- Press the **Xbox button** or **View + Menu** to switch between the game and Big Picture. The game is paused meanwhile.

### Remote Monitoring
Track the playtime of your console games from your **phone**.

- Switch it on in **Settings → Monitoring** and scan the **QR code** with your phone.
- Pick a game on your phone and start or stop the monitoring. All screens of the PC go black meanwhile.
- Your home network must be set to *Private* in Windows.

### Screensetups
Switch your monitors and your audio device with one click, e.g. from *desk with two monitors* to *TV only, sound through the soundbar*.

### Automatic game import
Games you install with **Steam, Epic, Xbox, GOG, Ubisoft Connect or Rockstar** are added as profiles by themselves, with a cover. Switch it on in **Settings → General**.

## New features

- **Playthroughs:** a new application to create, edit, pause, complete and cancel playthroughs.
- **Tags:** managed in **Selection Lists**. Add your own tags and hide the ones you don't use. A game's tags are shown as cards in the profile.
- **Disc image:** choose which part of the cover is shown on the disc.
- **Dynamic background:** your Windows wallpaper or the cover of the selected game behind the app (*Settings → Appearance*).
- **Session cleanup:** sessions shorter than a minimum are not saved, e.g. a game started by mistake.
- **Blackout:** an animated clock on the black screens, a full blackout by hotkey for console games, and a per-profile setting for blacking out side monitors.
- **External filter** for the profiles.

## Improvements

- New look throughout the app: new icon, splash screen and animations.
- **Settings** are sorted into pages and can be searched.
- Improved dashboard and library filters.
- The playthrough overview shows the total playtime of each playthrough.
- The running game detection is faster.
- IGDB can be switched off in the settings.
- arcora can only be started once.

## Bug fixes

- **Launch game** is only available when the game can really be started.
- Games running in the background are detected reliably.
- Fixed a crash with certain game processes (e.g. WARDOGS).
- Fixed the change dates of saved data, the start arguments after a restart, choosing a cover from disk and the filters of the playthrough overview.
